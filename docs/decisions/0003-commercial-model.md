# 0003: Commercial model

- Status: accepted business policy; formal legal terms pending
- Date: 2 October 2026

## Context

FireCA should publish its source while retaining commercial revenue from organizational deployments and services. Government is an intended paying customer segment. Education and public-interest status must not automatically exempt an organization from paying.

## Decision

Use a source-available model with free individual personal non-commercial use and paid organizational use. Businesses, government, education, charities, NGOs, research institutions, and other organizations have no category-based free-use exemption.

Define third-party hosting/managed services, resale, and embedding rights separately from ordinary internal-use licenses. The official hosted service is offered by subscription.

Paid self-hosted deployments must support disconnected operation with locally verifiable signed license files.

## Consequences

This is not the earlier option of free internal organizational use with restrictions only on competing hosts. Stock PolyForm Noncommercial does not fit the accepted boundary. An adapted or separately drafted license needs accurate naming and an explicit personal-use grant.

The policy is documented, but a free-use license and commercial agreement have not been adopted. The initial copyright notice reserves rights pending formal terms; the repository must state this clearly.
